# Wellness Program

First Rehab's Wellness Program is the clinic's ongoing maintenance service line — a structured way for patients to keep the ground they gained in active physical therapy instead of losing it once formal treatment ends. It sits between "discharged with a home exercise sheet and a handshake" and full clinical PT: less intensive than acute rehab, more structured than going it alone. See [[00-Clinic-Overview]] for how this fits into the clinic as a whole.

## What's Included

| Component | Description | Typical Frequency |
|-----------|--------------|--------------------|
| Movement screen / check-in | Brief functional assessment to catch regressions or new issues early | Monthly |
| Guided maintenance sessions | Hands-on session reinforcing mobility/strength work from active PT | Ongoing, per plan |
| Home exercise program (HEP) updates | Progressed or adjusted exercise sets as the patient's baseline improves | As needed at check-ins |
| Injury-prevention coaching | Movement-pattern correction aimed at the activities that caused the original injury | Ongoing |
| Direct line to clinical staff | Ability to flag a flare-up or new issue before it becomes a full re-injury | As needed |

This is deliberately lighter-touch than acute PT — it's designed to be sustainable for a patient long after their insurance-covered visits or original treatment plan has ended.

## Who It's For

- **Patients discharged from active PT** who want structured support to avoid backsliding into the same injury.
- **Active adults and weekend athletes** looking for injury prevention rather than injury treatment.
- **Aging patients** focused on mobility maintenance, balance, and fall prevention.
- Anyone the clinic sees who doesn't need — or no longer needs — the intensity of acute-phase PT, but still benefits from a professional check-in cadence.

It is explicitly **not** a substitute for acute-injury physical therapy. A new injury, post-surgical recovery, or an active condition still routes into standard PT; the Wellness Program picks up after that phase, or runs alongside it for patients who want ongoing support beyond their prescribed visit count.

## How It's Marketed

- Featured as its own service on the public site — see [[03-Website-Content]] for the service-page inventory.
