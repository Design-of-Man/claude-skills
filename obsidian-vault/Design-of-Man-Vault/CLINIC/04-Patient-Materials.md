# Patient Materials

Patient Materials are operational and clinical-support content — the paperwork, handouts, and reference sheets used in the course of running the clinic day to day. This is a distinct category from marketing content: nothing here is written to attract a new patient, it's written to support one who's already in the door. For the marketing side, see [[03-Website-Content]].

## Compliance Note — Read First

**This vault does not store patient records or any protected health information (PHI).** This page catalogs material *types* and *templates* — the intake form itself, the FAQ handout itself, the boilerplate care-instruction sheet — not any individual patient's data. Real patient records, filled-out intake forms, and clinical notes live in the clinic's practice-management/EHR system, never in Obsidian, never in an agency tool, and never in a client-facing repo. If a specific patient's information ever needs to be referenced for clinic operations, it stays out of this vault entirely.

## Categories

| Material Type | Purpose | Format | Where Used |
|----------------|---------|--------|------------|
| New patient intake paperwork | Collects patient history, consent, insurance info ahead of first visit | Paper / patient portal form | Front desk, pre-visit |
| Insurance & billing FAQ handout | Answers common billing/coverage questions | Printed handout | Front desk, pre-visit |
| Post-visit / home exercise program (HEP) sheet | Reinforces exercises assigned during a session | Printed or app-based handout | Handed out at end of session |
| Discharge / Wellness Program transition handout | Explains the shift from active PT to the maintenance program | Printed handout | Discharge conversation (see [[01-Wellness-Program]]) |
| Condition-specific care info sheets | General care/education info for common conditions treated | Printed handout | As relevant during treatment |

## Distribution

Primarily handed out in person at the front desk or at the end of a session; where the clinic's patient portal supports it, digital versions are also made available. Distribution method is a clinical-operations decision, not a marketing one.

## Boundary With Marketing Content

The agency's role stops at the marketing layer — website copy, FAQ page content, blog posts, podcast production (see [[03-Website-Content]] and [[02-Podcast-Schedule]]). Patient Materials are owned and maintained by clinic clinical/front-desk staff, not by the agency. When in doubt about which side of the line a piece of content falls on: if it's meant to bring someone to the clinic, it's marketing; if it's meant to support someone who is already a patient, it's a Patient Material.

## Ownership

Clinic clinical and front-desk staff maintain content and accuracy of these materials (dosages, exercise instructions, condition information). The agency does not edit clinical content — only the surrounding marketing material described in [[03-Website-Content]].
