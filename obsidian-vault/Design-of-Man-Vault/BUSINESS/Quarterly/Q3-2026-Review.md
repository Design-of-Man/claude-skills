# Q3 2026 Review (August – October)

The first quarterly review note for The Design of Man. Q3 2026 is
explicitly a baseline-setting quarter per [[../01-Financials]] — this
note exists to capture what "baseline" actually meant going in, what's
true partway through, and to leave a clean, honest placeholder for what
gets filled in once the quarter closes.

## Goals Set at Quarter Start

Pulled directly from [[../01-Financials]] as of quarter start:

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| Quarterly objective | Establish baseline (not a revenue or client-count target) |
| Year-end client target (context) | 10 clients |
| Revenue per client | $X / month |
| Monthly expense baseline | ~$155 (see [[../03-Expenses]]) |
| KPI baseline to establish | Conversion rate, CAC, revenue/client, profit margin, time-to-build, time-to-launch — see [[../04-KPIs]] |

Because Q3 is a baseline quarter rather than a growth-target quarter,
"success" here means having real, measured numbers behind every `$X`
and every KPI in [[../04-KPIs]] by the end of October — not hitting a
specific revenue figure. Q4 2026 (5–8 clients, $X revenue) is the first
quarter with an actual growth target, per [[../01-Financials]].

## Mid-Quarter Checkpoint — As of August 2026

**Status: early quarter, infrastructure phase.**

As of this checkpoint (August 18, 2026), the quarter's actual work has
been standing up the operational infrastructure this baseline depends
on, rather than running client work against fully measured KPIs yet:

- This Obsidian vault ([[../00-Company-Overview]] and everything
  linked from it) is being built out as the system of record for
  clients, financials, agents, and automations.
- The automation calendar ([[../../AUTOMATIONS/00-Automation-Calendar]])
  and the agent specs ([[../../AGENTS/00-Agent-Overview]]) are being
  documented and, per their individual status fields, moved from
  "Planning" toward "Ready."
- Integrations that KPI measurement depends on — particularly
  [[../../INTEGRATIONS/05-QuickBooks-Online]] for revenue/expense/margin
  numbers — are being connected.
- The pricing structure ([[../02-Pricing-Model]]) and capacity model
  ([[../06-Capacity-Planning]]) that Q4's growth target depends on have
  been reasoned through and documented.

This means most of the KPI table in [[../04-KPIs]] does not yet have a
real measured value — the "how to measure" methods are defined, but
Q3's actual baseline numbers are not in hand at this checkpoint. That's
expected for a quarter whose stated goal is establishing the baseline,
not hitting one.

**What to check at the next mid-quarter-style pass (mid-September):**
whether automations have moved from "Ready" in spec to actually running
on schedule, and whether the first real numbers (client count, first
month of QBO data) are available to start populating [[../04-KPIs]] and
[[../01-Financials]] with real figures instead of `$X` placeholders.
