# Abacoa Podiatry

## Overview
Podiatry practice serving the Abacoa/Jupiter, FL market and one of The Design of Man's flagship live builds — the site a lot of our later medical-practice work borrows patterns from. Full-service foot & ankle care, with the site built to give the practice a fast, mobile-first web presence and online scheduling front and center, backed by a content engine to keep local SEO fed.
