# Jupiter Laser & Regenerative Medicine

## Overview
Foot & ankle and laser regenerative medicine practice in Jupiter, FL, run by Dr. Orlando Cedeno, DPM. The practice's core differentiator is MLS (Multiwave Locked System) laser therapy — a non-invasive treatment used for plantar fasciitis, Achilles tendonitis, and arthritis. This is a live, actively-managed client, and the one with the heaviest ongoing content workload on the roster — less "build and maintain," more "build and keep feeding."

## Site / Domain
- **Domain:** jupiterlaser.com
- **Host:** Vercel — see [[../INTEGRATIONS/02-Vercel]]
- **Repo note:** the codebase lives in `Design-of-Man/abacoapodiatry` — it was originally cloned from the Abacoa Podiatry template repo as a starting point and the repo name was never updated. It's functionally unrelated to [[01-Abacoa-Podiatry]]'s own deployment; just a naming holdover worth knowing before you go looking for a `jupiterlaser`-named repo that doesn't exist.

## Status & Timeline
- **Status:** Live, stable

- Full site build (Next.js, design system)

## Content / SEO Notes
- Active blog content program focused on MLS laser therapy education and myth-busting: what it treats (plantar fasciitis, Achilles tendonitis, arthritis), how it differs from surgery or injections, and correcting common patient misconceptions about laser therapy being "not real medicine" or "just heat."
- The angle works for both SEO (long-tail queries like "does laser therapy work for plantar fasciitis") and patient trust-building ahead of a first visit.
- Runs on the standard Wednesday content/graphics cadence plus this dedicated blog workstream — see [[../AGENTS/03-Copy-Content-Agent]] and [[../AUTOMATIONS/00-Automation-Calendar]].
- Good candidate for AI-search / answer-engine optimization given how much of the content is inherently myth-busting Q&A — worth checking this hasn't already been left off the AI SEO pass.
