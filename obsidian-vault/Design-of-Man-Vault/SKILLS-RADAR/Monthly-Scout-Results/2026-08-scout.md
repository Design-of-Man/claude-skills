## Off-Cadence Addition: Domain Migration Skill

This vault-build session itself surfaced one more real addition beyond the
three scheduled finds above: **domain-migration**, a Wix/Squarespace →
Vercel cutover checklist skill, was actually built and added directly to
the team's `claude-skills` repo on **2026-08-18** — mid-session, rather
than waiting for the next scheduled scout run.

That's a deliberate exception to the normal flow (watchlist →
evaluate → queue → adopt), and it's worth being explicit about why:

- The gap wasn't hypothetical. While reviewing recent sessions for
  patterns, a recurring pattern turned up — repeated Wix/Squarespace →
  Vercel domain cutovers (RegenOrtho, First Rehab) handled ad hoc each
  time, with no shared checklist.
- It had already caused a near-miss: a prior migration needed to fall back
  from Firecrawl (unavailable) to a plain HTTP crawl for post-cutover
  verification, and that fallback wasn't written down anywhere before this.
- The gap was concrete and recurring, not speculative — exactly the case
  where spending queue time on [[../01-Repos-to-Evaluate]] first would
  have been process for its own sake. Build-then-log made more sense than
  evaluate-then-build for something this narrow and this clearly needed.
