# Clinic Content

Reusable and archived content assets for **First Rehabilitation** — podcast show notes, FAQ content, and wellness program materials that have already been produced and can be repurposed elsewhere (blog posts, social captions, patient handouts, prospect audits used as a "here's what good clinic content looks like" reference).

## This folder vs. CLINIC/

This is the **library view**, not the source of truth. Scheduling, production status, and the authoritative copy of what First Rehab publishes live in the `CLINIC/` section:

- [[../../CLINIC/02-Podcast-Schedule]] — the actual podcast production calendar: episode topics, recording dates, Descript editing status, publish dates. If you need to know what's airing next or what's already aired, go there.
- [[../../CLINIC/04-Patient-Materials]] — the authoritative set of patient-facing materials (intake forms, wellness program handouts, FAQ sheets currently in circulation at the clinic).

Once an episode has aired or a patient material has been finalized, the *reusable* pieces of it — a good pull-quote, a FAQ answer that keeps coming up, a wellness-program explainer that could become a blog post — get archived here so they can be repurposed without re-digging through the clinic's operational files.

## What lives here

| Content type | Source | Reuse examples |
|---|---|---|
| Podcast show notes | Finished episodes, pulled from [[../../CLINIC/02-Podcast-Schedule]] once aired | Blog post per episode, social clip captions, email newsletter blurb |
| FAQ content | Recurring patient questions, cross-checked against [[../../CLINIC/04-Patient-Materials]] | Website FAQ page, chatbot/intake copy, social "did you know" posts |
| Wellness program materials | Program handouts once finalized at the clinic | Landing page copy, lead-magnet PDF, [[../03-Blog-Ideas]] source material |

## Status

No archived assets yet — this folder is standing up ahead of the first podcast episodes airing under the current [[../../CLINIC/02-Podcast-Schedule]] cadence. Populate it as episodes and materials clear the clinic's own pipeline, not before — content gets archived here once it's finished and live, not while it's still in draft.
