# Team Overview

The Design of Man is a two-person agency: **Nick Kashuba** and **Cam**. Everything in this vault — client builds, agents, automations, the prospect pipeline — runs through one of the two of them. There is no larger org chart to document; the point of this section is to make the split between the two of them explicit, so approvals, client contact, and ownership don't have to be re-negotiated every time.

## The Team

| Person | Full Profile | High-Level Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Nick Kashuba | [[01-Nick-Profile]] | Technical build & deployment, agent/skill tooling, internal ops |
| Cam | [[02-Cam-Profile]] | Prospect outreach, sales pipeline, client-facing communication |

Full detail on who owns what — including where ownership is shared and where it's still an open question — is in [[03-Roles-and-Responsibilities]].

## How Work Splits, at a High Level

The clearest signal for how work is actually divided comes from the prospect assignments in the pipeline: Nick is the named point of contact for the Revital IV prospect, and Cam is the named point of contact for IV League and New Life Peptides. That pattern — Nick weighted toward the technical/build side, Cam weighted toward sales and client-facing work — is used throughout this section as the working model for the team, not as a rigid policy either of them has signed off on.

Beyond named accounts, most of the agency's actual output (site builds, agent runs, social content, SEO changes) is produced by the agents documented under [[../AGENTS/00-Agent-Overview]] and scheduled under [[../AUTOMATIONS/00-Automation-Calendar]]. Nick and Cam's day-to-day work is less "build the thing" and more "review, approve, and stay the point of contact" — see [[../AUTOMATIONS/Gates-and-Approvals]] for where a human has to sign off before anything ships.
