# Cam

This is a working profile, not a bio — it exists so that "who do I ask about X" has a clear answer. It covers focus areas, what Cam owns, and what he's the point of contact for. Personal detail beyond that isn't tracked here.

## Focus Areas

- **Client-facing communication** — the first line of contact on the accounts below; fielding replies, scheduling demos, and keeping the prospect tracker current (see [[../COMMUNICATIONS/03-Prospect-Tracker]]).
- **Sales content review** — alongside Nick, reviewing the Sales Outreach Agent's drafted emails before they send (see [[../AUTOMATIONS/02-Monday-Sales-Outreach]]) and the weekly social content before it goes to clients (see [[../AUTOMATIONS/03-Wednesday-Content-Gen]]).

## What He Owns

- The client-facing side of the sales approval gate: anything the Sales Outreach Agent drafts for these two accounts gets his review before it ships (see [[../AUTOMATIONS/Gates-and-Approvals]]).
